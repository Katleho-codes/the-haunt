# Kiroween competition steering

- Target category: Costume Contest. The product is a useful personal commitments manager with a polished, coherent and unforgettable cinematic horror interface.
- The official project submission period is 30 October through 4 December 2026. Build all submitted project work inside this period.
- Keep `.kiro/` at the repository root and ensure it is committed; never add it to `.gitignore`.
- Do not attribute code or workflows to Kiro unless they were actually performed with Kiro. Keep the Kiro usage log and evidence accurate.

- Do not claim features, tests, integrations or delivery states that have not been verified.
