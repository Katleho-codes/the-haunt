# Product steering — THE HAUNT

## Product purpose
THE HAUNT is a personal commitments and life-admin manager. It helps people capture, organise and remember responsibilities such as household bills, subscriptions, renewals, appointments, school-related dates and personal tasks.

## Product promise
Keep life's loose ends from coming back to haunt you.

## Target market
Start with South African needs (including ZAR and locally familiar obligations) without hard-coding South Africa into the product. Support multiple currencies, user locale and timezone.

## Product principles
1. Capture before complexity: a commitment should be quick to enter.
2. Confirm before committing: extracted dates, amounts and recurrence must be reviewed.
3. Remind without overwhelming: users control their reminders and sound preferences.
4. Atmosphere without sacrificing usability: horror is the visual language, not a usability obstacle.
5. Useful after Halloween: no core functionality may depend on seasonal novelty.
6. Honest product states: never imply a reminder was delivered, an AI model was used, or a document was processed when that has not happened.

## Scope discipline
Do not introduce banking, payments, subscription cancellation, social feeds, autonomous financial actions or full project management without explicit product approval. Prefer a complete narrow product over a broad unfinished platform.
