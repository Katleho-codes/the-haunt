# Visual atmosphere — requirements

## Goal
Create a distinctive cinematic supernatural-horror product experience without damaging usability or accessibility.

### Requirement 1 — Visual identity
- The UI SHALL use the approved palette, type hierarchy, environmental layers and consistent surface styles.
- The mansion environment SHALL be a coherent visual signature, not a random asset collage.

### Requirement 2 — Functional clarity
- WHEN a user interacts with a control THEN its purpose and state SHALL remain visible and understandable.
- Important text SHALL remain legible over visual artwork.
- Warning colours SHALL be reserved for real warnings.

### Requirement 3 — Sound
- Audible playback SHALL be disabled by default.
- Users SHALL be able to independently enable or disable ambient and interaction sound.
- Sound SHALL not be required to understand completion, warning or navigation states.

### Requirement 4 — Motion
- The interface SHALL respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Ambient effects SHALL be slow and non-obstructive.
- Controls SHALL never move unexpectedly or be hidden by effects.

### Requirement 5 — Responsive behaviour
- The main application SHALL work on desktop and mobile viewports.
- Mobile navigation SHALL be keyboard and touch accessible.
